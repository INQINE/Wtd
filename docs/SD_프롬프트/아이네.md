# 아이네 SD 프롬프트

**올릴 이미지 (순서대로)**: ① 아이네 사진 1 ② 아이네 사진 2 ③ 명일방주 SD 레퍼런스 시트
**앱**: Gemini 앱 / AI Studio / ChatGPT (무료)

```
Images 1 and 2 show the same character. Image 3 is a style reference sheet of chibi battle sprites.
Create ONE chibi battle sprite of the character from images 1-2, drawn in exactly the same art style as image 3.

Character (keep these details):
- silver-white hair with a faint lavender sheen, long hair loosely tied in a low ponytail with loose strands,
  side-swept bangs falling over one eye, one ahoge (antenna hair strand) on top
- gentle half-lidded violet-magenta eyes, soft calm smile, light blush
- outfit: white ribbed turtleneck sweater, cropped black jacket with techwear straps and buckles,
  purple pleated skirt, dark brown tights, chunky black boots
- small silver star-shaped pendant necklace with a blue gem
- role: medic. Holding an oversized ornate healing staff with a glowing star-shaped crystal head, silver and violet

Style (match image 3 closely):
- super-deformed chibi, about 2.5 heads tall: big wide head, compact body, short sturdy legs
- full body, wide combat-ready stance with knees slightly bent, 3/4 view facing right
- thick dark brown-black outlines with a slightly rough hand-drawn line quality
- two-tone cel shading, sharp glossy highlights on the hair
- muted, slightly desaturated palette with violet as the accent color
- large vertical oval eyes with gradient irises and a small white highlight, tiny mouth

Output:
- exactly one character, centered, entire body visible including feet, margin around it

Background (important — the image will be cut out automatically):
- PREFERRED: export as a PNG with a REAL transparent background (alpha channel). Do NOT draw a gray/white checkerboard pattern.
- If a real transparent background is not possible: fill the ENTIRE background with one flat solid color #00FF00 (pure green),
  no gradient, no shadow, no glow on the background. If the character has green in it, use #FF00FF (pure magenta) instead.
- The background color must also fill every gap inside the silhouette (between arms and body, between hair strands,
  inside rings or holes of the weapon). Never leave white fill in those gaps.
- no ground shadow, no text, no border, no frame
```

> 아이네는 흰 머리·흰 스웨터라서 흰 배경이면 누끼가 지저분해진다. 반드시 투명 PNG 또는 초록 단색 배경으로 받을 것.

**게임에 넣은 명령 (현재 그림, 흰 배경이라 틈 영역을 직접 지정)**
```bash
npm run sd:import -- .sd-raw/ine-gemini-1.webp ine --tolerance 24 \
  --hole-region "0.70,0.27,0.93,0.50;0.275,0.43,0.295,0.45;0.563,0.464,0.583,0.484;0.358,0.476,0.378,0.496;0.572,0.483,0.592,0.503;0.336,0.527,0.356,0.547"
```

**결과가 어긋나면 이어서 보낼 말**
- 그림체가 다르면: `Match image 3's thick outlines, flat cel shading and chibi proportions more closely.`
- 안 닮았으면: `Keep the silver-white hair, the bangs over one eye and the violet eyes exactly like images 1-2.`
- 발이 잘리면: `Show the whole body including feet with margin.`
- 체크무늬가 그려지면: `That checkerboard is drawn, not transparent. Redo it on a flat solid #00FF00 green background instead.`
- 틈이 하얗게 남으면: `Fill the gaps between the arms, hair and staff with the same green background color too.`

## (보관용·사용 안 함) 공격 모션 시트 (ChatGPT / Gemini)

> 2026-11-13: 그림 1장 + 코드 시전 모션이 더 좋아서 그 방식으로 확정. 아래 시트 프롬프트는 참고용으로만 남김.

**올릴 이미지**: 게임에 들어간 아이네 SD 그림 1장
**권장 크기**: 정사각형 (1024×1024) → 2×2 칸에 4프레임

```
Using the attached chibi character as the exact model, create a 4-frame ATTACK ANIMATION sprite sheet.

Layout:
- one square image split into a 2x2 grid of equal panels, read left-to-right, top-to-bottom
- NO grid lines, NO borders, NO numbers, NO text — just plain pure white background everywhere
- the SAME character in every panel: identical face, hair, outfit, colors, staff design, line style and size
- every panel: full body visible including feet, facing right in 3/4 view,
  feet planted at the SAME spot near the bottom-center of the panel, nothing cropped, margin around the character

Frames (a medic casting a healing spell with her star staff):
1. Ready stance — same pose as the attached image.
2. Wind-up — leans back slightly and lifts the star staff high above her head with both hands, hair and skirt swaying back.
3. Cast — swings the staff forward and up; the star crystal bursts with soft violet light and a few small star sparkles
   close to the staff head, hair and jacket flowing forward.
4. Follow-through — staff coming back down toward the ready stance, a few fading sparkles.

Keep the same art style as the attached image: chibi proportions, thick dark outlines, cel shading.
```

**어긋날 때 이어서 보낼 말**
- 칸마다 크기가 다르면: `Make the character exactly the same size in all four panels, feet on the same baseline.`
- 잘리면: `Keep the whole staff and both feet inside each panel with white margin.`
- 테두리/숫자가 생기면: `Remove all grid lines, borders and numbers; white background only.`

**게임에 넣기**: 그림을 채팅에 올리면 Claude가 아래 명령으로 넣는다.
```bash
npm run sd:import -- <시트 그림> ine --attack --frames 4 --grid 2x2 --fps 10
```

## (보관용·사용 안 함) 공격 모션 8프레임

**올릴 이미지**: 게임에 들어간 아이네 SD 그림 1장
**크기**: 가로로 긴 그림 (1536×1024) → 4×2 칸 (위 4칸, 아래 4칸)

```
Using the attached chibi character as the exact model, create an 8-frame ATTACK ANIMATION sprite sheet.

Layout:
- one landscape image split into a 4x2 grid of 8 equal panels (4 on top, 4 on bottom), read left-to-right, top-to-bottom
- NO grid lines, NO borders, NO numbers, NO text — plain pure white background everywhere
- the SAME character in every panel: identical face, hair, outfit, colors, staff design, line style and size
- every panel: full body visible including feet, facing right in 3/4 view,
  feet planted at the SAME spot near the bottom-center of the panel, nothing cropped
- keep every pose, the staff and all effects fully INSIDE its own panel with white margin;
  nothing may cross into a neighboring panel
- the change between consecutive frames must be small and gradual, like in-between frames of a smooth animation

Frames (a medic casting a healing spell with her star staff):
1. Ready stance — exactly the pose of the attached image.
2. Anticipation — knees bend a little, weight shifts back, staff starts to rise.
3. Wind-up — staff raised to shoulder height, body leaning back, hair swaying back.
4. Peak — staff held high above the head with both hands, star crystal starts to glow faintly.
5. Swing — staff swinging forward and down, a short violet motion trail behind the star.
6. Cast — staff thrust forward, the star crystal bursts with soft violet light and small star sparkles close to the staff head, mouth open.
7. Follow-through — staff still extended forward, light fading, hair and jacket flowing forward.
8. Recover — returning to the ready stance, a few last tiny sparkles.

Keep the same art style as the attached image: chibi proportions, thick dark outlines, cel shading.
```

**어긋날 때 이어서 보낼 말**
- 칸마다 크기가 다르면: `Make the character exactly the same size in all eight panels, feet on the same baseline.`
- 동작이 갑자기 바뀌면: `Make the motion between frames 2-5 more gradual, like smooth in-between frames.`
- 이펙트가 옆 칸으로 넘어가면: `Keep all light effects inside each panel; nothing crosses the panel edges.`
- 테두리/숫자가 생기면: `Remove all grid lines, borders and numbers; white background only.`

**게임에 넣기**
```bash
npm run sd:import -- <시트 그림> ine --attack --frames 8 --grid 4x2 --tolerance 7 \
  --durations 0.06,0.07,0.08,0.07,0.05,0.13,0.12,0.12
```
