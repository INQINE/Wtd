## 대기 SD 프롬프트
**올릴 이미지 (순서대로)**: ① 징버거 사진 ② 명일방주 SD 레퍼런스 시트

```
Image 1 shows the character. Image 2 is a style reference sheet of chibi battle sprites.
Create ONE chibi battle sprite of the character from image 1, drawn in exactly the same art style as image 2.

Character (keep these details):
- bright blonde long straight hair past the shoulders, side-swept bangs partly covering one eye, one ahoge on top
- a small yellow triangle-shaped hairpin on the bangs
- red eyes, sleepy half-lidded calm look, expressionless small mouth, light blush
- outfit: black tailored suit jacket over a white collared shirt, with techwear straps, buckles and a utility belt added,
  black pleated skirt, black tights, chunky black boots
- role: guard (melee). Holding an oversized, highly detailed greatsword with a gold guard and yellow accent lines,
  held ready in front at an angle

Style (match image 2 closely):
- super-deformed chibi, about 2.5 heads tall: big wide head, compact body, short sturdy legs
- full body, wide combat-ready stance with knees slightly bent, 3/4 view facing right
- thick dark brown-black outlines with a slightly rough hand-drawn line quality
- two-tone cel shading, sharp glossy highlights on the hair
- muted, slightly desaturated palette with yellow/gold as the accent color
- large vertical oval eyes with gradient irises and a small white highlight, tiny mouth

Output:
- exactly one character, centered, entire body visible including feet, margin around it

Background (important — the image will be cut out automatically):
- PREFERRED: export as a PNG with a REAL transparent background (alpha channel). Do NOT draw a gray/white checkerboard pattern.
- If a real transparent background is not possible: fill the ENTIRE background with one flat solid color #00FF00 (pure green),
  no gradient, no shadow, no glow on the background.
- The background color must also fill every gap inside the silhouette (between arms and body, between hair strands,
  between the sword and the body). Never leave white fill in those gaps.
- no ground shadow, no text, no border, no frame
```

**결과가 어긋나면 이어서 보낼 말**
- 그림체가 다르면: `Match image 2's thick outlines, flat cel shading and chibi proportions more closely.`
- 안 닮았으면: `Keep the blonde long hair, the yellow triangle hairpin, the red sleepy eyes and the black suit exactly like image 1.`
- 발이 잘리면: `Show the whole body including feet with margin.`
- 체크무늬가 그려지면: `That checkerboard is drawn, not transparent. Redo it on a flat solid #00FF00 green background instead.`
- 틈이 하얗게 남으면: `Fill the gaps between the arms, hair and sword with the same green background color too.`

## 게임에 넣기
```bash
npm run sd:import -- <SD 그림> jingburger      # SD
npm run art:import -- <일반 그림> jingburger   # 메인 화면용 일반 캐릭터 그림
```
